**What to notice**

- Every item is an outcome («Decide who…»), not a topic («Help center»).
- The «Why it's here» column traces each item to the record, so nobody wonders who put it there.
- What last Tuesday left open is on the agenda without anyone copying it over: the help center owner, the pricing question (now settled), the vendor quote.
- Things that don't need the group's time went to «Async instead» and «Parking lot».
