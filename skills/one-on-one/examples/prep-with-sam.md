# Example: 1:1 prep with a frustrated engineer

**The setup.** Sam Lee, an engineer on the onboarding launch, has waited 15 days for legal to review a vendor quote. The folder holds the [action list](../../../demo/after/ACTIONS.md), the [decision log](../../../demo/after/DECISIONS.md) and [today's standup summary](../../standup/examples/launch-week-standup.md). There are no earlier 1:1 notes with Sam.

**Wednesday 18 Nov, late afternoon. Maya:** Prep my 1:1 with Sam tomorrow (Thu 19 Nov, 30 min). Sam sounded frustrated about legal in the standup. Last month he asked about working on billing integrations next quarter.

---

# 1:1 prep - Sam Lee, Thu 19 Nov (30 min)

There are no earlier 1:1 notes with Sam, so nothing carries over from last time. This starts his file.

## What happened since
- **Vendor quote:** in legal since 3 Nov, 15 days (A-003, Blocked). He pinged legal again yesterday and got no reply (standup 18 Nov). Your ask to the legal lead (A-002, due 17 Nov) still shows Open in `ACTIONS.md`. If you've made it, bring the answer. If not, it's worth doing before tomorrow.
- **His work now:** event tracking for the 10% rollout (standup 18 Nov), the first stage of D-001. The 8 Dec look at the numbers before going to 100% likely relies on it (check).

## Agenda
1. **His topics first** (~15 min). He hasn't shared any yet. Ask him today, for example: «For tomorrow's 1:1, anything you want to cover? Bring it or drop it here.»
2. **The vendor quote and legal** (~10 min). Not a status check: how the wait has been for him, what you're doing about it (A-002), and what he needs from you.
3. **Billing integrations next quarter** (~5 min). He asked last month. Tell him where it stands, or by when you'll know.

## Questions
- What's taking more energy than it should right now?
- Anything I could do differently that would help you?
- What project would stretch you?

Right now both topics on the list are yours, so give his the first half. Aim to leave with an agreement on each side, each with a date, for example yours on getting a review date from legal.

Saved to `one-on-ones/sam-lee.md`: billing integrations as a standing topic, and tomorrow's agenda. The file is private, and nothing went into shared files. Your note that he sounded frustrated isn't in it, since the doc keeps facts and agreements. Say if you want it recorded.

```markdown

```

**What to notice**

- The delay is legal's, and the prep doesn't treat it as Sam's fault. The topic is what he needs, not a status check.
- Maya's impression («sounded frustrated») shaped the agenda but stayed out of the file. 1:1 notes may be read later in a context nobody planned for.
- The prep points out that every topic so far is the manager's, and drafts the message asking Sam for his. It sends nothing.
