# Example: a Friday status report for stakeholders

**The setup.** By Friday the folder holds the week's record: Tuesday's [minutes](../../meeting-minutes/examples/weekly-sync.md), the [RAID log](../../raid-log/examples/start-from-minutes.md), the [decision log](../../decision-log/examples/log-and-ask.md) and the [updated action list](../../action-items/examples/friday-update.md). The PM's [notes for the week](../../../demo/updates-2026-11-20.md) are newer than the RAID log.

**Friday 20 Nov. Maya:** Write my Friday status update on the onboarding launch for stakeholders: Elena Brooks (VP Product) and Marco Diaz (Sales lead).

---

Saved to `reports/2026-11-20-onboarding-status.md`. It is written for Elena and Marco, about a page:

# Onboarding launch - status, 2026-11-14 to 2026-11-20

**Status: Green** - on track for the 10% launch on 1 Dec. One thing to watch: billing's new price IDs are planned for 26 Nov, one day before we need them (DP-001).

## What changed

- Decided with finance: the new prices go on the pricing page only after the new onboarding reaches 100% of new sign-ups, so not before 15 Dec (D-003).
- Done: QA on the new onboarding finished on 19 Nov; two minor bugs were fixed the same day. The rollout note went to support on 19 Nov (A-001).
- New risk: support overload in pricing week (R-001), covered by the staged launch.

A few notes before you send it:

- **Why Green:** nothing is late, and the one High risk (support overload, R-001) has a working mitigation in the staged launch. The margin on billing is one day. If the 26 Nov date slips at all, next week's report turns Amber.
- **«Needs from you» is empty** because the record doesn't show anything Elena or Marco has to do. The one candidate is the help center articles: if you want Elena's help finding an owner, I'll add it as an ask.
- **`RAID.md` still shows Tuesday's state.** The report uses your notes for the newer facts (QA done, legal review on 24 Nov, billing on 26 Nov, the finance answer), but I didn't change the log. Want me to bring it up to date?
- This is the first report in `reports/`, so there is no «was» status yet. Next Friday's report will compare against this one.

Want me to send it to Elena and Marco? No email or chat tool is connected here, so I'd format it as an email for you to paste.

---

**What to notice**

- The status follows written rules, not mood, and the reply says why it's Green and what would turn it Amber.
- The headline alone tells a reader what matters: on track, one thing to watch.
- Every line points to a record (D-, A-, R-, DP-), so a stakeholder who asks «says who?» gets an answer.
- «Needs from you» says «nothing» rather than inventing an ask.
- Newer notes beat the stale RAID log, and the report says so.
