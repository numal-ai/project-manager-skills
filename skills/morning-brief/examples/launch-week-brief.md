# Example: a morning brief in launch week

**The setup.** Maya Okafor is the PM of a new onboarding launch. Tuesday's weekly sync left [minutes](../../../demo/after/meetings/2026-11-17-weekly-sync.md), a [decision log](../../../demo/after/DECISIONS.md) and an [action list](../../../demo/after/ACTIONS.md) in her folder. Her calendar is connected: [Wednesday's meetings](../../../demo/calendar-2026-11-18.md). Nothing else is connected.

**Wednesday 18 Nov, 08:30. Maya:** Brief me.

---

# Wednesday 18 Nov - 3 things need you

## Decide (2)
- **Prices at launch or after?** Leo can't finish the pricing page copy without the answer (raised 17 Nov, no outcome; A-004). → 10:00 with Dana Ruiz
- **Help center articles** - who updates them for the new onboarding? No owner since the 17 Nov sync, where it was left to «figure out offline» (A-006, due 24 Nov (check)).

## Unblock (1)
- **Sam** - vendor quote in legal since 3 Nov, 15 days (A-003, Blocked). Your ask to the legal lead for a review date was due yesterday and is still open (A-002).

## Do (0)
- Nothing due today.

## Today's meetings
- 10:00 Pricing page timing, Dana Ruiz (finance) - get the at-launch-or-after answer Leo is waiting for (A-004, see above).
- 13:00 Billing check-in, Ana - when do the new price IDs ship? Needed by 27 Nov or the 1 Dec launch is at risk (A-005; risk in the 17 Nov minutes).
- Also today: 15:00 Design review of the onboarding emails, with Leo and design.

Not checked: tracker, email and chat (not connected); RAID.md (not in the folder).

Want a draft of the note to the legal lead for A-002?

---

**What to notice**

- Six open actions became three lines. Everything else waits, and the brief says what it didn't check.
- Maya's overdue A-002 is what blocks Sam, so it appears once, under Unblock, not twice.
- Each meeting line says what Maya needs to walk out with.
- Nothing was written anywhere. The brief only reads, and offers the next step.
